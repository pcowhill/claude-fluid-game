Harvest the Hazard

The hazard is your enemy — and your only fuel.

A glowing fluid floods out of the Emitter in the low basin and spreads downhill, pooling deep. You don't want to wipe it out — you want to farm a controlled flood.

  • Extractors turn fluid into energy. Deeper fluid = more energy — but too deep and the fluid destroys them.
  • Relays extend your network so you can reach distant ground. Everything must stay connected to the Core to work.
  • Blasters burn energy to push fluid back and protect your line.
  • Barriers are cheap walls — dam a basin to grow a deep, lucrative reservoir.
  • Beacon — build it in the deadly basin and keep it powered to charge it. Charge it to 100% to WIN.

You lose if the fluid submerges your Core Base. Keep it off the high ground!
